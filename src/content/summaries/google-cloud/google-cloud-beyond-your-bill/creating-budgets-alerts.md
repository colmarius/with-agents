An unnamed presenter frames budgets as scoped rules that notify teams when actual or forecasted cost crosses thresholds. Notifications and alerts do not impose a hard cap or stop usage. The 2019 UI, IAM, scope, threshold, notification, and cost-data behavior require current-doc review.

### Key Points Covered

- Budgets can monitor selected billing scope and notify on actual or forecasted thresholds, but do not cap spend. [00:00–01:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F4omjjMZ54k&t=0s)
- The historical feature supports fixed or prior-month-derived targets and multiple scoped budgets. [01:02–02:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F4omjjMZ54k&t=62s)
- Credit inclusion and multiple percentage thresholds change what is monitored and when teams are warned. [03:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F4omjjMZ54k&t=186s)
- The episode demonstrates actual and predicted period-end thresholds. [03:06–04:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F4omjjMZ54k&t=186s)
- Email and Pub/Sub notifications can trigger responses, while enforcement requires a separate, carefully tested control. [04:08–05:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F4omjjMZ54k&t=248s)
