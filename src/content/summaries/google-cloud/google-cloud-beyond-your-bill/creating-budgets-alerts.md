An unnamed presenter frames budgets as scoped rules that notify teams when actual or forecasted cost crosses thresholds. Notifications and alerts do not impose a hard cap or stop usage. The 2019 UI, IAM, scope, threshold, notification, and cost-data behavior require current-doc review.

### Key Points Covered

- Budgets can monitor selected billing scope and notify on actual or forecasted thresholds, but do not cap spend. [00:00:00]-[00:01:02]
- The historical feature supports fixed or prior-month-derived targets and multiple scoped budgets. [00:01:02]-[00:02:04]
- Credit inclusion and multiple percentage thresholds change what is monitored and when teams are warned. [00:03:06]
- The episode demonstrates actual and predicted period-end thresholds. [00:03:06]-[00:04:08]
- Email and Pub/Sub notifications can trigger responses, while enforcement requires a separate, carefully tested control. [00:04:08]-[00:05:11]
