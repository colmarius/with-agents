An invoice says what you owe; a billing report helps explain why the amount changed. This 2019 demo follows a cost spike from the total bill to a project, then to the particular BigQuery activity responsible.

### Key Points Covered

- **Start with the trend, then narrow the question.** The report compares current and prior-period spending and projects month-end cost from historical trends. That forecast is an estimate; changing the time range or filtering by location helps examine the pattern behind it. [00:00–02:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jRb8piwa2GI&t=0s)
- **Drill down until the charge points to an activity.** The demo identifies one project behind a spike, groups its spending by product, and then examines the SKU—the specific billable item. BigQuery analysis, the charge for running queries, explains the increase. Clear project names help connect the charge to the application to investigate. [02:06–04:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jRb8piwa2GI&t=126s)
- **Use the financial view for reconciliation.** Credit filters show the effect of discounts. The cost-breakdown report instead combines usage, discounts, and taxes to explain the final invoice amount, a different question from what caused a workload's spending to rise. [02:06–04:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jRb8piwa2GI&t=126s)
