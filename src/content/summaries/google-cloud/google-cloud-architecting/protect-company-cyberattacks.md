Palo Alto Networks' Google Cloud alliance technical lead Mason describes a 2022 endpoint-posture integration with Google's context-aware access controls.

### Key Points Covered

- Mason claims Palo Alto Networks' automation resolved 95% of alerts and reports its MITRE ATT&CK evaluation results. [00:00–04:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-4Ci-zs2bwE&t=0s)
- He says the first posture release checked agent presence, administrative isolation, and an alert-derived health threshold. [04:15–08:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-4Ci-zs2bwE&t=255s)
- A Cloud Function synchronized Cortex XDR posture into Google endpoint management for access-policy decisions. [08:26–10:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-4Ci-zs2bwE&t=506s)
- The Cortex XDR Endpoint Verification integration was explicitly in private preview, not generally completed availability. [10:33–11:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-4Ci-zs2bwE&t=633s)
- This 2022 vendor account is not a reusable reference design; confirm current documentation and availability, workload-test failure modes, and obtain independent threat-model, security, privacy, data, and compliance review.
