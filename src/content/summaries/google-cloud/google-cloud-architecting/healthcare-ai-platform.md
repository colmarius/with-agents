Vida Health vice president of engineering Amol describes Vida's 2021 consolidation of application, analytics, workspace, and machine-learning workloads, including provider-assistance concepts.

### Key Points Covered

- Amol attributes cost and developer-productivity benefits to consolidation and reports an approximate cost reduction. [00:00–03:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QjMl9lgcOU0&t=0s)
- He describes assistive note-taking, caseload prioritization, and protocol recommendations. [03:10–07:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QjMl9lgcOU0&t=190s)
- Multiple health-data sources landed in BigQuery; Vertex AI use was only exploratory. [07:22–10:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QjMl9lgcOU0&t=442s)
- Migration was completed with SADA, but Cloud Healthcare API and FHIR storage were future plans. [16:54–21:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QjMl9lgcOU0&t=1014s)
- This 2021 customer account is not a reusable reference design; verify current documentation and workloads and require healthcare, clinical-safety, privacy, consent, compliance, interoperability, model-risk, data-governance, and security review.
