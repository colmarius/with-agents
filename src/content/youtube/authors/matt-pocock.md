## Cross-Playlist Synthesis

- Pocock's current [Agent Skills for Real Engineers overview](../playlists/matt-pocock-agent-skills/overview.md) frames skills as mostly user-invoked procedures for distinct phases—clarification, high-fidelity exploration, multi-session planning, implementation, review, collaboration, and setup—while version 1.2 allows Writing for Agents to be invoked by the model when relevant ([Grill summary](../videos/UzMNBN6xLLA/summary.md), [workflow summary](../videos/M6mYodf0dJM/summary.md), [Prototype summary](../videos/n0VhIVtviC0/summary.md), [Wayfinder summary](../videos/F3lL98Pj90o/summary.md), [review summary](../videos/mh5XZ-L5SFQ/summary.md), [v1.2 summary](../videos/gaDdrDdczO4/summary.md)).
- Pocock repeatedly keeps strategic control with the developer: the human bounds and steers interviews, decides when prose lacks enough fidelity, chooses routes through dependent decisions, and accepts or rejects review proposals ([Grill summary](../videos/UzMNBN6xLLA/summary.md), [Prototype summary](../videos/n0VhIVtviC0/summary.md), [Wayfinder summary](../videos/F3lL98Pj90o/summary.md), [review summary](../videos/mh5XZ-L5SFQ/summary.md)).
- Pocock treats context-sized work and durable handoffs as complementary. A task that fits one effective context can proceed directly, while larger work records a destination, bounded tickets, decision provenance, or collaborator answers so fresh sessions do not have to reconstruct alignment ([workflow summary](../videos/M6mYodf0dJM/summary.md), [Wayfinder summary](../videos/F3lL98Pj90o/summary.md), [v1.2 summary](../videos/gaDdrDdczO4/summary.md)).
- Editorial: The author's examples prioritize procedural control and inspectable artifacts over hidden autonomy, but the collection does not compare this workflow with alternatives or measure delivery speed, defects, maintenance cost, review capacity, or team outcomes.
